The owner approved softer junctions, uneven translucent clouds and sparse bright
threads, adding that Vastra should feel like a web connecting everything. The
production default now selects six living groups while retaining the 24-node
budget. A connected backbone plus bounded nearest-group links forms alternate
routes across the landscape. Those links attach to facing outer branches;
local tributaries stay acyclic so individual groves are not enclosed in cages.

The earlier visual support mask made connections almost disappear between
groves. Current Connection Visibility now supplies a bounded corridor along
actual inter-grove paths; unrelated empty terrain retains its quiet residual.
The revealed-field heatmap uses the same corridor calculation. Current Web

Three unequal veils overlap in depth, broader low-frequency texture makes dark
lanes, and fixed asymmetric cubic bends avoid straight wire-like spans. Endpoint
opacity and fine-strand emphasis are reduced while the surrounding haze carries
continuity. Unresolved distant star glints fade to avoid block-like fragments.
No simulation, affinity ownership, Sol behavior or player-radius rule changed.

## Decision

Stop tuning the broad ribbon carrier as the final visible form. Separate the
world's quiet refractive density from the sparse wisps that communicate flow.
Keep the approved cosmic-web morphology as the spatial organization: shared
regions around vegetation, narrowing connections between regions. The web does
not need to be continuously outlined. Wisps travel through it; the whole region
does not oscillate as a sheet.

The owner removed the player-centered manifestation radius. Regions, placement,
selection and density must come from world locations. Normal view culling and
streaming may bound rendering cost, but must not create a circular energy field
around the explorer. Preserve floating-origin conversion without using the
player's position as the effect's origin or density source.

## Local comparison and redistribution

Both Vefects licenses permit use/modification in games and prohibit publishing
their assets or derivatives in public repositories. Keep vendor content under
ignored `artifacts/vastra_resource_review/`. Do not copy vendor shader bodies,
textures or modified scenes into tracked source. Production adoption needs a
local asset preparation step and a build workflow that supplies those licensed
files privately; otherwise retain them only as visual references.

`tools/prepare_vastra_resource_review.py` extracts only the dependency closure
of the four selected scenes, preserves the supplied licenses and records archive
hashes. It rejects executable dependencies and unsafe paths. The independent
fixture `tools/review_vastra_resources.gd` loads no game code, saves, demo scripts,
plugins, audio or vendor compositor. It compares each effect over the same ground
and landmarks; local wind process-material overrides shrink the showcase spawn
volume from 100 × 20 × 100 to 12 × 4 × 12 and remove its ten-unit height offset.
This is a resource comparison, not an integrated or accepted Vastra replacement.

Godot 4.7.2 Forward+ / Direct3D 12 imported the selected dependencies and rendered
all four effects without final-run engine errors. Captures and 24-frame sequences
per effect are in the ignored comparison folder; logs are
`artifacts/vastra-resource-import.log` and `artifacts/vastra-resource-render.log`.
The first fixture launch exposed a local script type-inference error, corrected
before the successful runs. The ownership check passed 151 mapped scripts/UIDs.

Inspected stills show that the normal wind flow is broad, soft haze/streaks,
not a finished cosmic web; stock settings would risk repeating the blanket look.
The trail still is too faint to assess reliably, so it is not selected as a final
solution. Distortion 06 is very quiet in this fixture; 11 includes a conspicuous
yellow accent that should not enter ordinary Vastra. These findings support a
layered prototype, not wholesale adoption of any stock scene. Animation captures
are available, but no performance acceptance or final motion approval is claimed.

## Revised implementation gates

1. Inspect the supplied effects in motion and compare their silhouette,
   intersections, direction and refraction. Select the useful layers before
   rebuilding the gameplay renderer.
2. Replace explorer-based selection with world-region ownership. Sample existing
   concentration and tile affinity; vegetation supports visibility, Sols dissipate
   it. Movement of the explorer must not change a fixed region's density or phase.
3. Add a small budget of directional wisps through stable world-space paths.
   Transition broad regions into finer connections without exposing solid sheets.
   Bind primary/complementary optics from the existing local-affinity owner.
4. Add quiet refraction separately, with soft geometry intersections. Keep sparse
   signals distinct from background movement. Avoid white fragments and double
   silhouettes. Test slope, side view, day/night and dense vegetation.
5. Measure CPU rebuild cost, GPU frame time and transparent coverage against an
   effect-disabled baseline. Cap work per geographic region and globally; use
   ordinary view culling. Retain distinct raw-concentration and visual-density
   heatmaps for diagnosis.

- Reference review: compared supplied wind/distortion frame sequences (frames
  0/8/16 in `artifacts/vastra-resource-motion-review.png`). Normal flow supplies
