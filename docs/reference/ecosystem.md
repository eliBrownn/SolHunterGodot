# Ecosystem — life, circulation and response

Review companion to the [Worldbuilding Bible](SolHunter_Worldbuilding_Bible.md),
November 1, 2026. This organizes existing decisions and evidence; it establishes
no new ecology rules. Approved design, current implementation and unfinished
work remain distinct. The picture atlas reuses the Plants review's stable IDs
and images, with separate current isolated captures where available.

## A world alive without the player

Nature should remain interesting without nearby Sols, NPCs or an authored quest.
Plants, trees, Vastra currents, small Vessel wildlife, sound and weather provide
ordinary activity. A quiet habitat can be healthy; movement and brightness alone
do not prove danger. The player's role is to observe, learn, cooperate and choose
how to affect a place, rather than make every natural event happen.

The visual relationship map in the HTML separates energy movement from biological
relationships. Its arrows describe existing direction, not universal recipient
rules, exact rates or a complete implemented food web.

## The inhabitants and their roles

| Element | Recorded role | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Grass and ordinary foliage | Emanate/accumulate Vastra and contribute to local circulation | Visible clumps share canonical membership; more blades do not create more energy |
| Trees and other reservoirs | Store received energy and relay surplus through actual outlets and roots | Tree capacity is separate from ambient concentration; the Bible records the 500/1,000 capacity conflict |
| Warden vegetation | Protection/defensive response | Authored paid actions, not permanent aggression or a universal requirement for healthy nature |
| Mender vegetation | Local repair | Repair consumes accounted energy; exact balance remains provisional |
| Regulator vegetation | Flow/signal moderation | Responses must settle rather than create endless self-triggering alarms |
| Vessels | Small Vastra-born non-Sol wildlife; usually swarm-like, feeding on plant Vastra and participating in response | Particle count is presentation, not a multiplication of consumers or stock; identity/lifecycle details remain open |
| Sols | Living individuals interacting with habitats; root surplus can feed them | Class/species and individual affinity remain independent; hunger/starvation rules are not implied |
| Humans | Use Vastra for civilization and interact with the ecology | Sustainable ambient harvesting is safe; harmful disruption is distinct from ordinary use |

Growth form, circulation role, specialist class and species are separate
properties. A tree can have a class and still stockpile. Ordinary vegetation
need not belong to a specialist class. Healthy places do not require all three.

## Circulation, feeding and repair

Plants accumulate stored Vastra gradually. Actual transfers travel between
anatomical endpoints; arrival credits recipients rather than generating a second
copy of the outgoing energy. Baseline nearby-tree selection is unbiased by need.
Root surplus, repair expenditure, Sol feeding and in-flight energy remain separate
accounts. Isolated plants and surplus tree root stock can use the recorded
finite-speed ambient-overflow path when their actual admission rules allow it.

Ambient concentration caps at 100. Incoming excess remains in conserved transfer
or backlog until it can be accepted elsewhere. Ordinary fullness and overflow do
not create ecological overload. A low stored reserve can follow ordinary spending
and is not evidence of injury. Reserve-driven leaf saturation and posture are
presentation of that state, with wind layered independently.

**Worked example:** a plant has no eligible nearby tree and exports an admitted
packet. A full ambient tile cannot silently discard the excess: it continues
through the finite transfer/backlog system. The scene may look energetic while
remaining healthy. This example adds no new transfer amount or reward.

and [paced intake/outlet](../vastra-integration.md#slower-source-output-and-paced-intakeoutlet--october-31-2026).

## Signals, disturbance and recovery

Approved direction includes paid small information pulses before full reserve,
local needs/warnings, bounded Vessel recruitment and response, reassessment,
expiring alarms and settling. Information never creates energy or establishes
plant consciousness. Removing a disturbance permits signal settling; ecological
damage, existing overload and permanent battle imprints have their own lifetimes.

Persistent disturbance can drive sustained response, aggressive release,
ecological overload and environmental storm risk. This is a causal design chain,
not proof that the complete signal language and lifecycle are implemented.
Storm formation is not guaranteed. Ordinary feeding, swarming, weather and safe
overflow are not independent storm causes. Battle storms use a separate lifecycle.

**Review example:** distinguish an ordinarily busy patch from an alarmed one,
then observe whether it settles after the actual disturbance ends. A bright swarm
by itself is insufficient evidence. Exact signal vocabulary and escalation
thresholds still require their owning implementation/authoring work.

## Flowers, fruit and the longer life of a habitat

Approved direction: trees produce fruit for Sol feeding and local regeneration.
Vessels consume Vastra from plant resources, cause flowers on other plants through
their activity, and pollinate ready fruiting tree sections by a real visit. The
first version does not require cross-tree pollen tracking. Actual fruit production
and Vessel feeding each have accounted costs; cosmetic passes are not free fruit.

Plant death can follow old age or sustained damage, with long tree lifespans.
Low reserves alone are not immediate death. Growth/flowering, fruit production,
pollination lifecycle, mortality and replacement remain unfinished authoring/
implementation. Captured plant models do not prove those behaviors exist.

Known distant forests are intended to continue through slower regional updates
during active play, sharing state with detailed nearby simulation. This broader
service remains open delivery. Explicit pause stops progress; no offline progress
is implied. Weather-based activity needs actual authored behavior, not visual rain.
