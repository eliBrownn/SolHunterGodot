## Habitat, routine and companionship

Bible direction: leaf litter; cleaning joints and arranging shelter material. The explicit trail placement is a playtest sighting,
not final biome distribution and not a change to world generation.
Signature/companion action: **Closes its front hooks like a clasp**. The owned idle branch
`claspback_moment` cues that model's signature using the existing rest/moment
timings. Wild behavior reuses patrol, idle and guard with neutral
temperament. No digging, shelter construction, inventory gifts, mind reading,
trust rewards or ecological terrain changes are added by these gestures.

Traversal is **ground only** in this slice. Wings, veil tissue, draconic ancestry
or unusual anatomy do not grant flight, swimming, phasing through scenery, mounts
or new encounter permissions. Conservative ground clearance is 0.65 m radius /
0.8 m height; these are connected safety bounds for the current small model.
