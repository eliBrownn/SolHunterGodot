# Visual direction: pixel art in a 3D world

Date: October 26, 2026. Status: owner-selected direction.

The sprite presentation is superseded by the owner's
[October 27 full 3D direction](2026-10-27-full-3d-and-controls.md).

The owner approved continuing with the 3D prototype, then clarified: preserve
the 2D look. Use real spatial depth, ground/bank geometry, and the lower
35-degree camera with zero sideways rotation. Keep an orthographic projection,
nearest filtering, original pixel-art terrain and prop textures, upright sprite
characters, and upright pixel foliage. Preserve authored atlas slices and pivots.

This selects a visual direction, not final production assets, a new generator,
physical locomotion rules, or full milestone acceptance. The earlier flat view
remains a reference. Smooth primitive trees and flat-color tile fields were
exploratory placeholders and are not the intended default aesthetic.

Next presentation priorities are consistent pixel scale across sprites and 3D
surfaces, readable occlusion around foliage/buildings, water/bank styling that
matches the art, and authored building footprints/collision. Performance budgets
and manual journey acceptance remain separate gates.
