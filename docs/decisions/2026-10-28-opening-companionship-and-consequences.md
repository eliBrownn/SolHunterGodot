# Opening, companionship, and lasting consequences

Date: October 28, 2026. Status: explicit owner direction and authorized
documentation update following the creative questionnaire and its corrections.
This records design intent; it does not claim implementation or playtest acceptance.
The [Bible](../reference/SolHunter_Worldbuilding_Bible.md) remains the design authority
and [project standard](../PROJECT_STANDARD.md) remains the workflow authority.

## Confirmed direction

- Lead the opening with unease and mystery. The player wakes in a Vastra storm,
  tries to escape, and is guided to safety by three Sols. One of those actual
  individuals becomes the starter. The rescue and later companionship must retain
  that individual's identity rather than replace it with a newly generated Sol.
- The starter shows curiosity and personality while accompanying the player.
  Shared travel should establish attachment before the legendary choice.
- Patient, nondestructive, respectful behavior supports earning a wild Sol's trust,
  analogous to earning an animal's trust. Capture/holding authority remains
  separate from affection and cooperation; specific trust formulas are not set.
- Civilization needs Vastra to run daily life. Human dependence and the effects
  of obtaining/using it provide the first ecological conflict's thematic basis.
  Sustainable ambient harvesting remains compatible with healthy habitats.
- Routine combat should reward strategy, leveling, training, journal discoveries,
  and lore within a satisfying creature-collecting adventure loop. The owner
  describes the ambition as "classic Pokemon 2.0"; no new reward trigger, farming
  rule, encounter duration, or numerical balance is established by that phrase.
- Affinity orbs are placeholders. Each Sol species ultimately has its own model,
  behaviors, and animations; individuals retain their own identities and affinity.
  This clarifies the temporary scope of the October 27 orb presentation without
  requiring immediate replacement of the working prototype.
- Refusing the starter's legendary request can still lead to victory. Winning
  demonstrates that the original Sol was capable without ascension; ascension
  would have made the path easier. Refusal permanently forfeits that starter's
  legendary manifestation in that save and also has lasting relationship and
  story consequences. The owner first selected difficulty/access alone, then
  explicitly withdrew that limitation and accepted the broader consequence
  direction. Do not retain the withdrawn answer as the operative decision.
- Preserve the irreversible choice against later in-game collection/separation
  loopholes. The original starter cannot die. Exact consequences and any
  protection against restoring/editing external save files remain undecided;
  no account service, online requirement, or anti-cheat system is approved.
- AYN Thor remains the release target. Choose the configuration with the largest
  verified unit sales when reliable evidence is available. No bestselling
  configuration has been verified by this review; model and performance budgets
  remain open rather than being guessed from order numbers or popularity.

## References and provisional interpretation

The owner's named references are Monster Hunter, Pokemon, Pokemon Legends:
Arceus, and The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. Their exact contribution
to SolHunter's visual/gameplay identity has not been individually ratified.

Candidate interpretation for future review: Monster Hunter for creature/habitat
presence and preparation; Pokemon for collection, attachment and growth; Legends:
Arceus for observation/approach; Tears of the Kingdom for curiosity, environmental
readability and interacting systems. These mappings are proposals, not additional
canon, permission to copy assets, or approval to import their mechanics.

## Approved follow-up — tutorial and visual identity

Owner approved the daily questionnaire summary and journal proposal on October 28,
then specified this campaign opening sequence:

1. Escape the Vastra storm with the three guiding Sols. Teach movement, sprinting
   and the basics of getting out of a storm through play, retaining unease and
   mystery. Exact exposure rules, failure/checkpoint handling and lesson prompts
   remain to be specified; this does not approve a stamina system.
2. After escape, meet the NPC who owns those Sols. They mysteriously left their
   owner to rescue the player; why they did so remains unexplained. The owner
   permits the player to keep one. Interactions suggest a bond, followed by the
   player's explicit choice. Retain that same rescuer's persistent identity.
3. That NPC gives the player their first protective mask for Vastra storms and
   teaches the basics. The owner's dictated "mass"/"Foster storms" is interpreted
   here as mask/Vastra storms, consistent with the established equipment canon.
   Protection efficacy, exposure thresholds and transfer details are not finalized;
   do not infer immunity or consumable filter/oxygen upkeep.
4. The NPC provides a first teaching battle to prepare the player for wilderness
   survival, followed by a journey to find the first refuge camp. The opponent,
   battle setup, rewards and loss/retry behavior still need authoring.

Strong stylized shapes and clear species silhouettes unify the approved full-3D
presentation. Protective refuge infrastructure first demonstrates civilization's
Vastra dependence; human order versus living wilderness defines the route's
contrast. Review a playable short route spanning refuge, habitat and encounter.
The existing refuge-return slice remains a development sample, distinct from the
campaign's initial storm-to-first-refuge sequence.

The first journal discovery reveals an observed Sol behavior with a useful habitat
clue, connecting personality, curiosity and tactical understanding. No automatic
stat or combat bonus is implied. Routine victory should primarily reward feeling
that the player understood the opponent and terrain. For refusal, explore a
changed but enduring relationship with the starter while preserving the permanent
legendary lockout; exact lasting consequences remain open. Rescuer distinctions
and the starter's first authored curiosity moment remain undecided.

## Open authoring and mechanics gates

- Storm origin/revelation, escape layout, actual exposure effects, failure and
  checkpoint behavior, rescuer species/personalities, NPC identity, mask efficacy,
  first teaching battle, and the exact interaction/confirmation presentation.
- The starter's first authored curiosity moments and the respectful interaction
  sequence that makes earned trust readable without repetitive upkeep.
- Exact lasting relationship/story consequences of refusal, choice warnings,
  and any external save-rollback policy. Do not infer numerical trust loss,
  random command refusal, additional deaths, or wider ecological damage.
- Routine/major battle durations and authored journal/lore rewards.
- Final species assets, interpretation of the reference games, and a verified
  Thor sales leader with measured device budgets.

## Delivery implications and authority

Keep the existing refuge-to-wilderness-and-return route usable for connected
acceptance while authoring the opening. The storm rescue is the approved campaign
opening target, not a claim that the current game already begins with it or an
instruction to replace unrelated active work. Evaluate travel, guidance,
companionship, observation, encounters, combat and durable outcomes together.

The Bible permits authored narrative storms to bypass procedural admission's
3x3 footprint. This opening does not change procedural storm rules, turn ordinary
rain into a Vastra storm, or make natural Sol activity originate overload.
Storm consequences still require their own authored specification.
