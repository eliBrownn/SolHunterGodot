# Sol flight and swimming

October 28, 2026. Connected wild-expedition traversal for the pilot trio.
Crown-Ear has bounded low flight; Reedback walks, wades and surface-swims;
Bellroot retains ground movement. Capabilities come from species content,
independently of each individual's affinity.

## Play and review

- Open [the species pilot](../scenes/sol_species_pilot.tscn) with F6 to review
  Crown-Ear near the trail. Its normal bounded routines can now take off and fly.
- Open [the pond pilot](../scenes/sol_traversal_pilot.tscn) with F6 to start a fresh
  seed-17 review beside Reedback's generated pond bank. This scene changes the
  review starting position, not the ordinary game's spawn. Later loads retain
  their saved player position.
- Give the wild Sol room to resume its normal routine. Flying and swimming do
  not require its individual affinity to be Air or Water.
- Approach a flying or swimming Sol to request a return toward land, then
  approach again when grounded. A blocked landing/bank causes a safe wait or
  alternate bounded route; it never teleports to fabricate a battle arena.
- In [the model workshop](../scenes/sol_model_workshop.tscn), M also cycles to
  Flight and Swimming. Select the bat or amphibian model respectively. These are
  isolated pose exercises, without navigation or species admission.

## Movement authority

Species records expose a `SolTraversalProfile`: ground, flight or swim, with
flight root altitude, maximum swim draft, conservative radius/height, and maximum
bank step. Capability is snapshotted when a wild individual is admitted. Later
authoring edits affect new admissions rather than rewriting a saved individual.
Profile values have finite bounds and runtime validation.

The saved roaming row holds the admitted policy, mode, mode-start tick, previous
cell and request to settle. Expedition state is version **15**; save envelope
format remains **3**. The current terrain generation version belongs to the
world-generation engine. This work does not import or replace that generator.

| Creature | States |
| --- | --- |
| Crown-Ear | ground → takeoff → air → landing → ground |
| Reedback | ground → water entry → wading/swimming → shore exit → ground |

Takeoff and landing validate vertical clearance and reserve the current cell.
Air travel stays on bounded cardinal paths within the existing home envelope.
The root tracks actual land/water height; clearance reserves space below the
origin for wing downstrokes. Swimming follows the real pond surface, while
shallow edges use supported wading instead of placing the body below the bed.
Entry/exit includes a small cosmetic lift whose overhead space is included in
the corridor check. Diving, riding and player swimming are not added.

The live controller gathers conservative bounds from actual published scenery
meshes. Unknown/unpublished geometry causes a wait. Swept body/appendage volumes
must clear scenery, sampled terrain and existing barriers; actor reservations
remain in the route search. Solid obstacle cells are still conservatively
excluded from flight even when their visual shape might offer a gap. This is
bounded low flight, without free steering or arbitrary obstacle bypass.

The geometry bounds are cached when chunk batches finish, including meshes
outside their decorative draw distance. A completely clipped shoreline batch is
skipped when it has no vertices; this avoids invalid empty-surface uploads.
Water itself, land/shore classification and elevations remain generated by Godot.

The pond review uses a bounded deterministic bank search near its trail anchor.
It inspects generated water and a legal adjacent dry home; it neither paints a
pond nor moves an existing identity. If no bank exists within the authored search
radius, that placement is omitted. Use a fresh review expedition after changing
placement settings. The ordinary pilot retains its original trail homes.

## Animation, persistence and encounters

Procedural flight blends wingbeats, elbow/wrist motion, tucked hind legs,
movement pitch and turn banking. The wing membrane follows the actual articulated
elbow/wrist/tip positions. Swimming blends limb strokes and tail steering around
the surface waterline. Rest/ground support solving remains connected on land.
The same models retain independent affinity effects and ordinary combat poses.

Transition height and free-movement phase derive from the saved active clock and
mode-start tick. Pause freezes that clock; save/load reconstructs the same phase
without rerolling identity or affinity. Culling and camera rebasing do not grant
movement or generate a route. Save validation accepts supported air/water cells
for the corresponding mode and rejects invalid mode/policy combinations.

Automatic ground encounters exclude airborne/waterborne actors. Manual approach
requests a return to land while exploration keeps running. Once grounded, the
existing legal-arena preparation, capture formula, moves and class passives apply.
The settlement request gives a bounded pause on land, rather than suppressing
all future activity. Observation remains available through the existing system.

This pass implements **wild traversal**. The subsequent
[owned companion integration](owned-companion.md#owned-flight-and-swimming-integration)
reuses its clearance, transitions and presentation for owned follow routes.
Tactical battles retain ground routing. Capturing removes the wild roaming state;
neither pass adds aerial combat, movement immunity or a new encounter range.

## Authoring and testing

See [Creating another Sol model](creating-sol-models.md). Add a traversal profile
to the species record only after its rig and envelope are ready. Ensure the
radius/height covers every animated appendage and supported art scale. Wingbeat
Rate and Swim Stroke Rate on a creature root control actual pose timing; they do
not change route speed, battle movement points or navigation permission.

`tests/sol_air_water.gd` exercises capability admission, malformed-state rejection,
launch/landing and aborted landing, real water entry/exit, blocked scenery,
ground/player restrictions, save-file IO, resumed transition phase, model posing,

actual game views. `tools/review_sol_air_water.gd` captures close workshop views.
